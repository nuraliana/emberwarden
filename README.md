**Emberwarden** is a 2D twin-stick arena shooter that runs entirely offline in a desktop browser. You are the last ember-keeper, defending the Lantern of Dawn against the Gloom: waves of motes, wisps, spitters and brutes close in from the dark, and after every cleared wave you choose one of three ember upgrades — split shots, piercing light, ember novas, thorn lanterns, faster dashes. A boss waits at wave 5 (the Gloom Matriarch) and wave 10 (the Hollow King); beat the King and dawn breaks — then the night goes endless.

Everything is original and made in code: the art is drawn on canvas, the sounds are synthesized with WebAudio, the names are its own. No files are fetched over the network and no build step is needed.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 — WASD or arrows to move, mouse to aim, hold click or Space to fire, Shift to dash, P to pause, M to mute.
